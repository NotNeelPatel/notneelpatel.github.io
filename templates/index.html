"In Today's World, People Are More Machine Than Machines Are."

A quote from Homunculus by Hideo Yamamoto that I think about often. I, too, have observed the increasing intersection in the venn diagram between humans and machines, which I find to be fascinating. Having studied engineering, I know how important it is to optimize. But to what extent do optimizations solve problems? I have seen the death of personality in websites, like nobody is actually here anymore. This website hopefully stands out from the rest. I encourage you to take some time to explore, read, and hopefully leave this website feeling inspired to create. Enjoy your stay.
